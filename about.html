My Journey

Turning Ideas Into Reality

I'm Sanket Kandel, a full-stack developer based in Gaindakot, Nepal — currently building high-impact products at the intersection of cloud infrastructure and modern web engineering.

My journey started in 2019 with a curiosity about how websites worked. What began as tinkering with HTML eventually became a deep passion for crafting production-grade systems. Today I architect solutions used by thousands of users daily.

I'm especially passionate about developer experience, open-source collaboration, and making complex technology feel effortless to use. When I'm not coding, I write about engineering on my blog, contribute to OSS projects, and explore the mountains nearby.
